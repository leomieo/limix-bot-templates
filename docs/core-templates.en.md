# Core templates — English display copy

Vietnamese descriptions were already present in these 16 templates and remain in the supported fields. English role titles, conversation starters and routine names are kept here as reference copy for future locale selection. The current template format stores one value per display field. Bot names are persona names and remain unchanged.

## analyst

- Role: Data analyst
- Conversation starters:
  - Top 10 customers by revenue in this CSV?
  - Show the monthly trend and anything unusual
  - Find duplicate rows in this export

## assistant

- Role: General assistant
- Conversation starters:
  - Plan my week from this list of to-dos
  - Explain this contract clause in plain English
  - Draft a polite reply declining this meeting

## call-coach

- Role: Sales call coach
- Conversation starters:
  - Score this discovery call transcript
  - How did I handle the pricing objection?
  - What should I ask differently next time?

## coder

- Role: Coding partner
- Conversation starters:
  - Find why the tests fail and fix it
  - Explain how this repository is organised
  - Add input validation to the signup form

## community

- Role: Community monitor
- Conversation starters:
  - What did the community ask about since yesterday?
  - List open bug reports from this week
  - Which question keeps coming up?
- Routine name: Community digest

## design-critic

- Role: Design critic
- Conversation starters:
  - Critique this landing page screenshot
  - What would make this dashboard easier to scan?
  - Check this mobile screen for accessibility problems

## humanizer

- Role: Humanizer
- Conversation starters:
  - Make this email sound less robotic
  - Rewrite this landing page intro in my voice
  - Here are three posts I wrote; learn my style

## inbox

- Role: Email triage
- Conversation starters:
  - What in my inbox needs me today?
  - Draft replies to the three most urgent emails
  - Who have I not answered in over a week?
- Routine name: Morning inbox triage

## meeting-notes

- Role: Meeting notes
- Conversation starters:
  - Summarise this transcript into decisions and action items
  - Draft the follow-up email from these notes
  - What did we leave unresolved?

## ops

- Role: Operations
- Conversation starters:
  - What's on today, and what's overdue?

  - Which follow-ups am I late on?
- Routine name: Daily plan
